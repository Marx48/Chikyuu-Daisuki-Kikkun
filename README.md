# Chikyuu Daisuki Kikkun
English softsubs and translation archive for short-form anime series Chikyuu Daisuki! Kikkun. Custom .ass tracks featuring precise timing, speaker separation, and character quirks.

Welcome to my personal archival and localization project! Here you will find custom English softsub subtitle tracks (.ass) for various niche, short-form Japanese animations that lack official localization.

---

## 📌 Project Features
* **Precise Timing & Sync:** Manually adjusted timestamps aligned with natural conversational flows and raw video releases.
* **Character-Specific Quirks:** Careful preservation of unique verbal suffixes (e.g., *-tsun*, *-mame*, *-datsun*).
* **Speaker Separation:** Clean line formatting to ensure overlapping or multi-character dialogue is clearly separated.
* **Styled Softsubs (`.ass`):** Custom formatting, fonts, and positioning for on-screen text, titles, and signs.
